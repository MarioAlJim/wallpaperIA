Weather Live Wallpaper — Privacy Policy

Effective September 6, 2026 · v1.0
In short

Weather Live Wallpaper collects nothing. It has no internet access, no accounts, no ads, and no analytics. Every setting and image you choose stays on your device.

01 What this app is

Weather Live Wallpaper is an Android live wallpaper that renders an animated storm scene (clouds, rain, lightning) or a day/night sun-and-moon scene directly on your device's home and lock screen, using your device's own graphics hardware.

02 Information we collect

None. The app does not request an account, does not connect to the internet, and does not include any advertising, analytics, or crash-reporting software development kit. There are no servers involved — nothing you do inside the app ever leaves your device.

03 Data stored on your device

The app saves the following, only in its own private app storage, so that your preferences persist between sessions:

DataPurposeLeaves the device?
Effect settingsRemembers your chosen cloud density, rain, wind, sun/moon, colors, etc.No
Custom background imageOnly if you pick one from your gallery as a scene backgroundNo
App theme & onboarding flagsRemembers Light/Dark preference and whether you've seen the tutorialNo

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of the above; there is no separate copy anywhere else, because none was ever made.

04 Photo access

If you choose to use your own photo as a background, the app opens Android's built-in system photo picker. You pick a single image yourself; the app never gets broad access to your gallery, and no photo is transmitted anywhere — it is simply copied into the app's own private storage on your device to be drawn as a wallpaper layer.

05 Sensors

With the optional gyroscope/tilt effect turned on, the app reads your device's motion sensors to shift the parallax of clouds and stars as you tilt the phone. These readings are used only in memory, frame by frame, to compute the animation — they are never stored or sent anywhere.

06 Permissions

The app requests no dangerous or sensitive Android permissions — no camera, microphone, contacts, location, or storage access. It declares only the standard system permission required to register as a live wallpaper, and the photo picker in section 4 works without any gallery permission at all.

07 Third parties

There are none. No advertising network, no analytics provider, no cloud backend, and no data broker of any kind receives information from this app, because the app never sends information anywhere in the first place.

08 Children's privacy

The app is not directed at children specifically, but since it collects no data from anyone, of any age, there is nothing to safeguard beyond what this policy already describes.

09 Changes to this policy

If this app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy — for example, by adding a feature that uses the network — this page will be updated first, and the version stamp in the masthead above will change accordingly.
